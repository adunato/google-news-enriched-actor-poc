Bridge repairs scheduled

Bridge repairs will begin in June after inspectors found corrosion in several support joints. The transport department said the work will close one lane at a time and maintain a route for emergency vehicles.

Contractors will replace damaged sections, apply protective coatings and test the structure before reopening each repaired span. Officials expect the project to last four months, depending on weather conditions and material deliveries.

Bus operators will publish temporary stop locations before the first closure. Pedestrian access will remain available, although people should follow signed routes around the active construction area.

Inspectors will examine the bridge after each stage and publish a short safety report. The department will announce any schedule changes through its public travel information service.
