Researchers map coastal flood risk

Researchers released a coastal flood map that identifies neighborhoods facing the greatest risk over the next two decades. The analysis combines tide gauges, elevation measurements and updated storm projections.

The team said local planning decisions can reduce damage when maps are paired with drainage improvements and clear evacuation routes. It cautioned that the estimates describe risk across broad areas rather than predicting a specific storm.

Several councils plan to use the findings when reviewing building standards. Community groups requested more detail about the assumptions and asked for translated versions of the public summary.

The researchers will update the map as new measurements become available. They also plan workshops with emergency services and residents during the coming year.
