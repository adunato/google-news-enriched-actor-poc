Harbor plan approved

City officials approved a long-term harbor restoration plan on Tuesday after months of public meetings. The project will replace aging barriers, restore wetlands and improve protection for nearby neighborhoods.

Engineers said construction will happen in stages so shipping can continue. The first phase will reinforce the eastern seawall, while crews monitor water levels and wildlife throughout the work.

Residents asked the council to publish regular progress reports. Officials agreed to release quarterly updates and to hold another public meeting before the second phase begins next spring.

The approved budget includes funds for independent environmental testing. The council said any major schedule change will require a public vote and a revised cost estimate.
